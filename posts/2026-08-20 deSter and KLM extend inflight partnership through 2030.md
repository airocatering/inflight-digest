deSter and KLM Royal Dutch Airlines have extended their longstanding partnership through 2030, 
continuing a relationship that has lasted more than four decades. The agreement reinforces their shared focus on innovation, 
quality and improving the onboard passenger experience. 

“We are delighted to continue our longstanding partnership with deSter through 2030,” said Bas Gerressen, 
EVP KLM Inflight Services. Over the years, the two companies have worked together on inflight products and services, 
with the renewed agreement allowing their collaboration to continue as KLM develops its onboard offering.
