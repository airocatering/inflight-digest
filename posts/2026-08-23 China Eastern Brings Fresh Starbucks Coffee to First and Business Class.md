China Eastern Airlines has begun serving freshly prepared Starbucks Arabica coffee in first and business class, bringing café-style coffee to its premium cabins as part of a broader "aviation plus coffee" partnership with Starbucks and Nestlé.

The new service is more than a coffee upgrade. It is the latest step in a partnership between China Eastern, Starbucks and Nestlé that began in 2025 and connects inflight service, airport experiences and loyalty programs.

Passengers in first and business class can now order freshly prepared Starbucks Arabica coffee and selected customized beverages at cruising altitude.

The move gives China Eastern a recognizable global brand in one of the most visible parts of the premium passenger experience — the beverage service.

The partnership has already generated tens of thousands of new airline loyalty memberships and more than 100,000 cups of Starbucks coffee through joint campaigns.

For China Eastern, branded coffee adds another layer to its premium offering alongside upgraded food, seasonal menus and other onboard services. For Starbucks, the partnership extends the brand beyond its traditional cafés into the airline cabin.

The rollout also reflects a wider trend in Asian aviation, where airlines are increasingly using specialty coffee and recognizable international brands to differentiate their premium cabins.

China Eastern has previously highlighted Chinese culinary traditions and regional ingredients in its premium menus, including products associated with Yunnan coffee. Starbucks now adds an international dimension to that offering.

The choice to start with first and business class is also commercially logical. Premium passengers are more sensitive to the quality of food and beverages, while the smaller cabin environment allows the airline to manage service consistency and passenger feedback before considering a broader rollout.

If successful, the program could eventually expand to other cabins.

For China Eastern, the strategy is clear: make the onboard experience feel less like an airline service and more like a premium lifestyle experience — starting with coffee.
