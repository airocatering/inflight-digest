Austrian Airlines makes coffee feel like more than just an onboard drink. 
Its Viennese coffee-house tradition brings a little bit of Vienna into the cabin, with classics like Melange and Einspänner.

It’s simple, distinctive and very Austrian — exactly the kind of small detail that can make a flight more enjoyable.
