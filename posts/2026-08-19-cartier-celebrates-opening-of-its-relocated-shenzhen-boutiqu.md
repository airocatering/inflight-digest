Cartier has reopened its boutique at Shenzhen Bao'an in the domestic departures hall of Terminal 3, operated with China Duty Free Group. The redesigned spacecarries a champagne-gold façade with undulating patterns drawn from the city'scoastline

Airport retail keeps drifting from counters to addresses. A brand with its own store in a terminal is no longer renting shelf space between flights — it is treating the airport as a high street, and travellers as customers rather than passengers.
