Etihad Airways has selected Paxia Cloud, an AI-enabled airline catering management platform, to modernize its catering operations.
The airline will implement the full Paxia Cloud suite covering meal specifications, galley planning, scheduling, ordering, invoicing, asset management and analytics.

The cloud-based platform will provide Etihad with a centralized system for its global catering operations, replacing legacy capabilities and reducing manual processes. 
The solution is designed to improve efficiency, data visibility, automation and collaboration across Etihad’s catering teams and partners.
