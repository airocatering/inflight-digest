Starting in October, KLM will introduce Grand Café KLM, a new onboard service concept on European flights offering passengers more choice and flexibility. 

Travelers will be able to pre-order meals such as a poke bowl or Caesar salad, or purchase items from the onboard menu, including a salmon egg wrap and yogurt with fruit and seeds. Complimentary coffee, tea, water, orange juice and a stroopwafel will remain available.
