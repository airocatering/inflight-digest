MacroAsia Earns P38-Million Dividend from Cebu Pacific Catering Services Investment Daily Tribune

MacroAsia Corp. is drawing fresh returns from its aviation services portfolio after its Cebu-based catering associate declared P95 million in cash dividends, giving the company a P38-million payout from the venture.
