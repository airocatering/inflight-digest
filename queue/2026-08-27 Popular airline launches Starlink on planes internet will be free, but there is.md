Popular airline launches Starlink on planes: internet will be free, but there is a condition Судово-юридична газета

German airline Lufthansa has completed its first flight on a plane equipped with Starlink satellite internet. The new system was installed on an Airbus A320neo with registration number D-AINM, which operates flights across Europe.
