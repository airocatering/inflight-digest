United says Starlink WiFi is driving new business Travel Weekly

United Airlines claims that installing Starlink WiFi is attracting customers and may help achieve a 10% pre-tax profit margin by 2027. The airline has installed Starlink on 43% of its fleet, planning to increase this to the entire fleet by next year.
