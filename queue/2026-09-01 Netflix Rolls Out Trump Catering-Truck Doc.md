Netflix has quickly turned the bizarre story of Donald Trump allegedly leaving Turkey aboard a decoy plane after riding in a catering container into a documentary. ABC News first reported the incident on August 13, saying Trump used the unusual route on July 8.

Just two weeks later, Netflix has already turned the story into a full documentary—an unusually fast turnaround for a story involving a presidential catering truck, a decoy plane, and a very unconventional escape.
