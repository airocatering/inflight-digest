Coming this month: Fast, free Wi-Fi to expand across Delta’s trans-Pacific network for full global connectivity Delta

Beginning this month, fast, free Delta Sync Wi-Fi presented by T-Mobile will roll out across Delta’s trans-Pacific routes. Once activated, the expansion will bring the final major region of Delta’s global network online for fast, free Wi-Fi.
