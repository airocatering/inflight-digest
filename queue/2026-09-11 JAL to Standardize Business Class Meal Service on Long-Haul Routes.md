JAL to Standardize Business Class Meal Service on Long-Haul Routes TRAICY Global

Japan Airlines (JAL) will standardize its Business Class inflight meal service style from September 1 on routes to the United States (excluding Hawaii and Guam), Europe, the Middle East, and Sydney.
