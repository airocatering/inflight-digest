Elon Musk has issued a new warning to Delta Air Lines after the airline chose Amazon’s LEO satellite service instead of SpaceX’s Starlink for in-flight connectivity.

Delta CEO Ed Bastian told Bloomberg that the airline chose Amazon Leo thanks to an “improved bandwidth with a much lower price point than what we’ve ever seen from Starlink.”
